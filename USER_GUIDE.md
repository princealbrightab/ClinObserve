# ClinObserve User Guide

This guide explains how students, professors, and HODs use ClinObserve in day-to-day academic workflow.

## 1. Overview

ClinObserve is a department-based clinical learning system. It gives students a place to:

- review patient cases
- record observations
- upload clinical images
- track case activity
- view departmental feedback

It gives HODs a place to:

- manage student and professor accounts
- assign students to professors
- create and maintain cases
- review student observations
- write private faculty feedback
- monitor department performance and reports

Professors can view assigned students and their observations, images, and feedback, and add faculty suggestions. HODs retain full existing department-wide administrative access.

Students share the academic case library and observation timelines, but each student's full biodata and private feedback have narrower access. Professors can see only their assigned students' records, including when several students observe the same patient.

## 2. Access and login

### Student login

1. Open the application login page.
2. Enter your assigned email and password.
3. If your account is newly created, you will be required to change your password before continuing.
4. After login, you are taken to your dashboard.

### Professor login

1. Sign in using the email and temporary password supplied by the HOD.
2. Change the temporary password when prompted.
3. Open **Students** or **Assigned students** from the dashboard to see your assigned learners.
4. If no students appear, contact the HOD to confirm your assignments.

### HOD / HOS login

1. Sign in with the HOD account.
2. You will be directed to the HOD dashboard.
3. From here you can manage students, professors, assignments, and learner activity.

### Password recovery

- Account holders may use the password reset flow if email recovery is enabled.
- If email recovery is disabled, the user must follow the HOD process for credential reset.

## 3. Student user guide

### Student dashboard

The student dashboard gives an overview of:

- recent activity
- own encounters and counts
- patient case activity
- calendar activity
- recent feedback entries

From the dashboard, you can navigate to:

- My observations
- Patients
- Calendar
- Profile

### Profile management

Students can update personal, non-administrative information in their profile, such as:

- phone number
- date of birth
- gender
- address
- short bio
- profile image

Students cannot change administrative details like role, assigned professor, academic registration details, or other HOD-controlled data.

### Viewing patient cases

1. Go to Patients.
2. Use search to find a patient by name or case number.
3. Open a case to view the case timeline and all linked observations.
4. You can review the clinical context and the observation history for that patient.

### Recording an observation

1. Open the patient case.
2. Click Record Encounter.
3. Fill in the required observation fields such as summary, symptoms, examination findings, assessment, and learning notes.
4. Upload up to five images if needed.
5. Save the observation.

### Editing an observation

Students can edit their own observations only while the encounter is still unlocked.

A record becomes locked after certain protected actions such as:

- the first AI review request
- first faculty review

If the encounter is locked, a new encounter should be created for later follow-up instead of editing the old one.

### Image management

For a student-owned unlocked observation:

- add images
- remove images
- view images that are authorized for shared access

Images are private and are not open to the public internet.

### AI review flow

If AI review is enabled, a student may request a text-only educational review.

Workflow:

1. Open the encounter.
2. Choose AI review.
3. Review the de-identified text preview.
4. Confirm the content is safe to send.
5. Submit the request.
6. Wait for the generated educational feedback.

The AI review is intended only as educational support and must not be treated as medical advice.

### Calendar view

The calendar shows:

- monthly encounters
- patient dates
- student learning activity
- selected-day details

Students can only see their own calendar activity. Professors can view all their assigned students or select one assigned student; HODs can view department-wide calendars.

### Feedback access

Students can see faculty feedback related to their own encounters, but they cannot see private feedback on other students’ work.

## 4. HOD / HOS user guide

### HOD dashboard

The HOD dashboard is the main operational hub. It includes summary information such as:

- department activity
- number of students active recently
- recent encounters
- recent AI reviews
- students without faculty feedback
- monthly and date-based summaries

### Managing students

From the Students section, the HOD can:

- view all students
- search by name, email, or roll number
- create new student accounts
- edit student profile and academic data
- activate or deactivate accounts
- reset temporary passwords and revoke sessions

When creating a student, the HOD provides the required learner details and the initial credential is shared securely.

The student must change their password on first login.

### Managing professors

1. Open **Professors** in the HOD navigation.
2. Choose **Add professor**.
3. Enter the professor's full name, login email, and temporary password. Passwords require at least 12 characters with letters and numbers.
4. Select **Save professor** and share the temporary password securely. The professor must change it at first login.
5. Open the professor's profile to view assigned students, edit their name/email, activate or deactivate the account, or reset credentials.

Deactivation ends existing sessions and blocks access. It does not remove student assignments or delete reviews. Resetting credentials also revokes sessions and requires another password change.

### Assigning students to professors

1. Open **Students** and add a student or open a student's **Edit academic details** page.
2. Select an active account in **Assigned professor**.
3. Choose **Save student**.
4. To reassign the student, select a different professor. To remove the assignment, choose **Unassigned** and save.

Each student has at most one current professor, and each professor can supervise multiple students. An existing assignment to an inactive professor remains visible as **(Inactive)** and can be retained while editing other student details. New assignments must use active professors.

The current professor can view the student's existing and future observations and feedback. The previous professor loses access after reassignment or unassignment, including access to feedback they authored. Historical comments remain saved. HOD access is unaffected; an unassigned student's work remains available to HODs for review.

### Managing patient cases

The HOD can:

- browse all patient cases
- create new patient records
- edit case context when permitted
- review the timeline of observations tied to a case

A case may be edited by the original creator before it has any linked encounters. After an encounter is recorded, the case becomes shared and HOD-level control is required for changes.

### Reviewing student observations

The HOD can view any student’s observation records and the patient case timeline.

This includes:

- encounter details
- attached images
- student notes
- evaluation history

HODs can review clinical reasoning and academic documentation without rewriting the student’s content.

### Writing faculty feedback

HODs may add private feedback to an encounter.

This feedback is:

- visible to the student only when it belongs to their encounter
- visible to all HODs and the student's currently assigned professor
- hidden from unrelated students and professors

Only the author may edit a feedback entry. An assigned professor may edit their own entry while the student remains assigned. Edits retain creation and update timestamps, but do not retain the previous comment text; add a new comment when the previous wording should remain in the history.

### Reports and summaries

The HOD can use reports to review:

- monthly attendance activity
- number of encounters per student
- patient counts
- student participation trends
- department-wide activity ranges

These reports help identify engagement, coverage, and progress across the academic cycle.

### Department calendar

The HOD calendar can be filtered to:

- all students
- a selected student
- a selected month and date

This allows the HOD to see a department-wide view for learning activity, while keeping student-specific access restricted appropriately.

## 5. Professor user guide

### Assigned students

Open **Students** to search your assigned learners by name, email, or roll number. Open a profile to read academic and personal details, view the student's observations, or open their learning calendar. Student account settings and assignment controls are available to HODs only.

### Reviewing observations and adding suggestions

1. Open **Observations**, or open an encounter from an assigned student's profile.
2. Read the observation and its attached images.
3. Review existing faculty feedback and AI review history.
4. Enter a suggestion in **Your feedback** and select **Add faculty feedback**.
5. Open your feedback entry to edit it later while the student remains assigned to you.

The first faculty comment locks the student's observation and images. Professors add feedback without rewriting the student's observation. Professors may read other faculty comments on assigned students, but may edit only their own.

### Patient cases, dashboard, and calendar

The patient list contains cases observed by your assigned students. On a shared patient case, only assigned students' observations and images appear; counts and last-observed dates use the same restriction.

Your dashboard shows assigned-student activity and recent feedback. In **Calendar**, select all assigned students or one assigned student and choose a month or date. Department-wide reports remain HOD-only.

### Access limits

Professors cannot create or manage accounts, change assignments, create patient cases or student encounters, edit observations or images, or request AI reviews on a student's behalf. Opening a direct link to an unassigned student's record does not bypass these restrictions. A professor with no assignments has an empty student workspace.

## 6. Typical workflow examples

### Student workflow

1. Log in.
2. Open the patient list.
3. Select a case.
4. Record an encounter.
5. Upload images if required.
6. Save and review the observation.
7. Wait for or review faculty feedback.
8. If enabled, submit an AI review for educational support.

### HOD workflow

1. Log in to the HOD dashboard.
2. Create or manage student and professor accounts.
3. Assign students to professors and review academic activity.
4. Open a student encounter and assess the record.
5. Add private feedback.
6. Review dashboards and reports for department performance.

### Professor workflow

1. Log in and open the assigned student list.
2. Choose a student and open an observation.
3. Review the student's documentation and images.
4. Add faculty feedback.
5. Follow the assigned students' activity through the dashboard and calendar.

## 7. Security and access expectations

ClinObserve enforces role restrictions and authorization checks.

Important rules:

- Students cannot access another student’s private reviews.
- Students cannot edit another student’s records.
- HODs cannot impersonate students when creating observations.
- HODs are the only users permitted to manage student and professor accounts, credentials, and assignments.
- Professors cannot access unassigned students' profiles, observations, images, calendars, faculty feedback, or AI reviews.
- Reassignment revokes the previous professor's access; deactivation blocks the professor's account while preserving assignments and history.
- Private review and image content remains restricted to authorized viewers.

---

## 8. Responsibilities of users

### Students

- keep your login secure
- do not share patient images or private feedback externally
- confirm that content is de-identified before sending to AI
- use the platform only for learning and academic review

### Professors

- review only assigned students' work
- keep student biodata, images, and feedback confidential
- provide educational suggestions and review AI feedback critically
- contact the HOD about assignment changes

### HODs

- manage accounts responsibly
- ensure students are active and properly assigned
- supervise faculty feedback
- verify AI review safety and institutional compliance

---

## 9. Best practices

- Use meaningful, academic reflections in encounter notes.
- Keep case documentation de-identified and professional.
- Only submit AI review requests for text that has been checked for identifying details.
- Use the calendar and reports as regular academic monitoring tools.
- Do not treat AI-generated feedback as a final diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

## 10. Troubleshooting

### Cannot log in

- Check your email and password.
- Make sure your account is active.
- If this is your first login, complete the required password change.
- Contact the HOD if you have not received your account details.

### Cannot edit an encounter

- The encounter may be locked after AI review or faculty review.
- Only the owner can edit an unlocked observation.

### No patient appears in search

- Professors should confirm with the HOD that a student who observed the case is assigned to them.
- Search by case number or a different name variation.

### Missing feedback

- Ensure the observation was reviewed by an HOD or the assigned professor.
- Only authorized users can access private feedback.

### Assigned student is missing or access was denied

- Confirm that the HOD saved the assignment to your professor account.
- A reassigned or unassigned student is no longer visible to the previous professor.
- Ask the HOD to check your account status if you cannot sign in.
